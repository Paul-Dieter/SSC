Planning Packages

2026/27

Three levels of support: The Mini steps in four weeks before the day, The Half up to six months out and The Full up to twelve. All three include shared planning documents and up to 8 hours of coordination on the wedding day. Live budget, supplier, timeline and checklist tracking comes with The Full.

The Mini

You plan, we execute · up to 15 service hours

R10 000

  • We step in 4 weeks before the wedding
  • Planning handover of up to 3 hours, online or in person
  • Shared digital planning documents
  • Final wedding-day timeline, setup and breakdown schedules
  • Supplier confirmations about two weeks before the day
  • Professional coordination of your wedding rehearsal
  • Up to 8 hours of wedding-day coordination on site
  • Full-day logistics, timeline and MC cues
  • Breakdown, pack-up and supplier collection oversight

The Half

You plan, we guide · up to 30 service hours

R15 000

  • Planning support begins up to 6 months before the wedding
  • Everything in The Mini, plus additional planning support
  • Planning consultation & roadmap of what is booked and outstanding
  • In-depth planning meetings through the remaining decisions
  • Supplier sourcing support and recommendations
  • Design development for decor, furniture, florals and direction
  • Assistance finalising menus, layouts and timelines
  • Site visits where required within the included hours
  • Final supplier coordination and wedding-week logistics
  • Rehearsal coordination and up to 8 hours on the day

The Full

You plan, you enjoy · up to 50 service hours

R20 000

  • Full planning can begin up to 12 months before the wedding
  • Everything in The Mini and The Half, with complete planning support
  • Live planning documents: budgets, suppliers, timelines and checklists
  • Budget management, built and tracked with you
  • Supplier sourcing, comparison and booking assistance
  • Creative guidance on decor, florals, colour palettes and styling
  • Stationery guidance on design and quantities
  • Layouts and logistics: timelines, floor plans and table layouts
  • Wedding website & PDF invitation, basic digital management
  • Rehearsal, final coordination and up to 8 hours on the day
  • A welcome gift from SSC

Full details are in the package PDF.
