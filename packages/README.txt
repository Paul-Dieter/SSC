Prices are duplicated on the pages
----------------------------------
The prices in these PDFs are also typed into the pages themselves, so a price
change means editing both. The places to look:

  stationery.html  every <dl class="product-tiers"> (55 product cards) and the
                   four <article class="tier-card"> wedding packages
  floral.html      the ceremony and reception tier-cards, the add-ons
                   price-list, and the <p class="product-price"> on each card
  planning.html    the three planning tier-cards and the add-ons price-list

Notes
-----
- Filenames are case-sensitive on GitHub Pages. Keep them lowercase.
- The pricing PDFs are large (8-28 MB each) because they are image-heavy
  exports. If page load or repo size becomes a problem, re-export them at a
  lower image quality; nothing in the code needs to change.
