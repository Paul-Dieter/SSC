Dear friends & family,

We made this little website so you would have everything in one place, and so our mothers could finally stop answering questions on our behalf. Where to be, when to be there, what to wear and where to find a decent coffee the morning after: it is all here.

Mostly, though, we wanted to say thank you. Nearly all of you have been part of our story somewhere along the way. You were at the braai where it started, on the other end of the phone during the Cape Town year, or helping us carry a couch up three flights of stairs. P.S. Biscuit will be there. He has a bow tie. He is not taking questions.

how it started

Our Story

We met at a friend’s birthday braai in Menlo Park in March 2019. Theo was on the fire, and Amara was the only guest who offered to help, mostly because she didn’t trust him with the chops. We talked until the coals went cold, swapped numbers, and then didn’t message each other for two weeks because we were both waiting for the other one to go first.

Our first proper date was a coffee in Hatfield that turned into lunch, then a walk, then dinner. The next year Theo took a job in Cape Town, and we spent eighteen months on Sunday-night video calls and early flights booked on sale. When he moved home in 2022 we found a small place in Garsfontein with a garden just big enough for Biscuit, the rescue pup who has been in charge ever since.

Theo proposed on an ordinary Saturday in December 2025, on the trail where we walk Biscuit most weekends. He had carried the ring around for three weeks waiting for the perfect moment, and in the end asked halfway up the hill while Biscuit tried to eat a stick. Amara said yes before he had finished the question.

good to know

Questions

Can I bring a plus one?

If your invitation says “and guest”, absolutely, and the RSVP form will ask for their name. Otherwise we’re keeping it small, and we are so grateful you understand.

Are children welcome?

We love your little ones, but we are keeping this one adults-only, apart from our nieces and nephews in the bridal party. Think of it as a night off.

Will Biscuit really be there?

For the ceremony, yes, with the bow tie. He goes home with Theo’s brother before dinner, because he has strong opinions about lamb.

What if it rains?

The ceremony moves into the barn and nothing else changes. April in Pretoria is usually kind, and we have asked nicely.

When do you need our RSVP?

By 1 March 2027, so the caterers can plan. If something changes after you reply, just fill in the form again and the latest answer counts.
